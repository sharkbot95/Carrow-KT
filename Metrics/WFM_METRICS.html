Call Volume Forecasting

Upload historical interval data (minimum 4 weeks, no upper limit) — Date, Time, Calls, plus optional AHT, Occupancy, Shrinkage, Utilization. Five methods run automatically and are compared for accuracy where history allows.

Defaults to your full uploaded range — narrow it to exclude an unusual week or use only the most recent history.
Used only for whichever of AHT/Occupancy/Shrinkage/Utilization your uploaded file doesn't include — those get forecasted from history instead.
Growth compounds from the forecast start date — e.g. 2% weekly means week 2's forecast is ×1.02, week 3 is ×1.02², and so on. Leave at 0% for a flat (non-compounding) forecast.

What you will be able to do

Every outcome is practised with interactive data, a lab or a quiz.

Understand

  • What a forecast contains and who depends on it
  • Level, trend, seasonality, events and noise
  • Seasonal indexes and intraday profiles

Build

  • Break a yearly forecast into month, day and interval
  • Apply moving averages and exponential smoothing
  • Prepare history and handle special events

Measure

  • MAPE, WAPE and bias
  • What forecast error does to service level and cost
  • A feedback loop from actuals to the next forecast

Who this course is for

Aspiring forecasters

Anyone moving into WFM forecasting or planning.

Schedulers and real-time analysts

People who use forecasts every day and want to know where they come from.

Operations managers

Leaders who need to question and trust the numbers.

No. All data is illustrative.
